Who We Are

Welcome to the C.O.G Brass Band (Royal Priesthood) — a family of musicians dedicated to worshipping God through the power of sound. From rehearsals to powerful performances, we play with purpose, unity, and passion.

We are a ministry of the Church of God, using brass instruments to lift hearts and glorify the King through every note we play. Our band is more than just musicians — we are brothers and sisters in Christ, united by our calling to serve through music.

Join us as we honour our calling, lift hearts, and glorify the King through every note we play.
